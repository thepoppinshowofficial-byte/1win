Tournament format

How the 2026 World Cup works

The 2026 edition introduces a new expanded format for the first time since 1998 — here is how 48 teams become one world champion.

🌍
June 2026

Group Stage — 12 Groups of 4

48 teams split into 12 groups of 4. Each team plays 3 matches. The top 2 from each group plus the 8 best third-placed teams advance — 32 teams total reach the Round of 32.

⚡
Late June 2026

Round of 32

The first knockout round in World Cup history. 32 teams compete in single-elimination matches. Lose and you go home — win and you are in the last 16.

🔥
July 2026

Round of 16 · Quarter-finals · Semi-finals

The knockout rounds that define the tournament. Every match is win or go home. Upsets, drama, penalties — this is where World Cup legends are made.

🏆
19 July 2026

The Final — MetLife Stadium, New York

The greatest prize in football. Two nations. 90 minutes. One world champion. The FIFA World Cup Final at MetLife Stadium in front of 82,500 fans and a global audience of billions.

📈

Up from 32 teams in previous editions. More nations than ever get a chance to compete — with bigger representation from Africa, Asia and the Americas.

Common questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is the FIFA World Cup 2026 being held?+
The 2026 FIFA World Cup is hosted across three nations — the United States, Canada and Mexico. The USA hosts 11 stadiums including the Final at MetLife Stadium in New York/New Jersey. Mexico hosts 3 stadiums including the opening match at Estadio Azteca. Canada hosts 2 stadiums in Toronto and Vancouver.
How many teams are in the 2026 World Cup?+
48 teams will compete in the 2026 FIFA World Cup — up from 32 in previous editions. The 48 teams are split into 12 groups of 4, with the top 2 from each group and the 8 best third-placed teams advancing to the new Round of 32.
When is the 2026 World Cup Final?+
The FIFA World Cup 2026 Final is scheduled for 19 July 2026 at MetLife Stadium in East Rutherford, New Jersey (New York area). The stadium has a capacity of 82,500 and will host the most watched sporting event on earth.
What is the new World Cup format for 2026?+
The 2026 World Cup uses a new expanded format. 48 teams are divided into 12 groups of 4. The top 2 from each group plus the 8 best third-placed teams (32 teams total) advance to a new Round of 32 — the first time this round has existed in World Cup history. From there it is standard knockout football through the Round of 16, Quarter-finals, Semi-finals and Final.
Who are the favourites for World Cup 2026?+
Based on current form and squad quality the favourites include France, Brazil, Argentina (defending champions), England, Spain and Germany. The host nation USA will have strong home support. The expanded format gives more nations a realistic path to the knockout stages, increasing the chance of major upsets.
