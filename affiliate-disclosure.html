Affiliate Disclosure

Transparency about how 1Winn earns income and how that affects the content you read on this site.

Home›Affiliate Disclosure
Effective Date: 1 June 2025

Summary: 1Winn participates in affiliate marketing programmes. Some links on this site may earn us a small commission if you click through and take an action — at no extra cost to you. This never influences what we write or recommend.

1. What is affiliate marketing?

Affiliate marketing is an arrangement where a website earns a commission by promoting products or services offered by a third party. When you click a link on 1Winn and subsequently make a purchase, sign up for a service, or complete another qualifying action on a partner's website, we may receive a small payment from that partner.

This is a common and transparent way for free content websites to earn revenue without charging readers or relying solely on display advertising.

2. Which links may be affiliate links?

Affiliate links on 1Winn may appear in the following types of content:

  • Recommendations for sports platforms, apps and services
  • Links to streaming services for live sports coverage
  • References to fantasy sports and gaming platforms
  • Any content where we reference a third-party service by name with a direct link
  • Promotional guides and sports offer reviews

Where a link is part of an affiliate arrangement we endeavour to make this clear with a disclosure notice near the relevant content.

3. Does this affect our editorial independence?

No. Our editorial content is written independently. We do not accept payment to write positive reviews, alter our opinions, or promote products we do not genuinely believe are relevant and fair for our readers.

Affiliate commissions are earned based on clicks and conversions — not based on what we say about a product. We would write the same guides whether or not affiliate arrangements existed.

🏆 Our commitment to you

1Winn exists to give sports fans honest, free, high-quality guides. We will never compromise the accuracy or integrity of our content in exchange for affiliate income. If we recommend something, it is because we genuinely believe it is useful — not because we are paid to say so.

4. Responsible Gambling Notice

Some content on 1Winn may relate to sports betting, fantasy sports or online gaming. This content is intended for adults aged 18 and above only. We strongly encourage responsible gambling:

  • Only bet or play with money you can afford to lose
  • Set strict time and spending limits before you start
  • Never chase losses with bigger bets
  • If gambling is causing you harm, seek help from a qualified organisation in your country

5. No Additional Cost to You

Clicking an affiliate link on 1Winn does not cost you anything extra. The price you pay for any product or service is exactly the same whether you arrive via our link or any other source. Affiliate commissions are paid by the partner company from their own margin — not added to your cost.

6. Third-Party Sites

Once you click an affiliate link and leave 1Winn, you are subject to the terms, conditions and privacy policies of the third-party website you visit. We are not responsible for the content, practices or policies of any external site.

7. Changes to This Disclosure

We may update this Affiliate Disclosure from time to time as our partnerships change. Updates take effect immediately upon posting. The effective date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions about our affiliate arrangements or this disclosure, please reach out via our Contact page. We are happy to clarify any specific link or partnership.
